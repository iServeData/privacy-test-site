Consent Preferences Footer

This sample demonstrates a fixed footer used for privacy-related links and consent controls.

What to observe

  • The footer remains fixed to the bottom of the viewport and will be hidden behind the consent banner if no preferences made.
  • The footer links display hover styles but first two links are for decoration, only Cookie Preferences work.
  • The Cookie Preferences control is styled to match the footer links.
